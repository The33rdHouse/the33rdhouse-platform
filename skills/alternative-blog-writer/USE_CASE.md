# First Production Use Case

## Australian Network Comparison Content Engine

Use this skill to create verified, decision-focused comparison articles that attract Australian commercial search traffic and route readers into the Australian Network, AIB Hub services, Shopify products, or consultation offers.

### Best initial article cluster

1. Best AI Tools for Australian Small Businesses
2. Best Cloudflare Alternatives for Australian Startups
3. Shopify vs WooCommerce for Australian Businesses
4. Best Telegram Alternatives for Australian Communities
5. Best Accounting Software for Australian Small Businesses
6. Best AI Automation Platforms for Australian Companies
7. Best Website Builders for Australian Service Businesses
8. Best CRM Alternatives for Australian Small Businesses

### Why this is useful

- It creates search-led traffic instead of relying only on social distribution.
- Each article can feed a matching Telegram vertical such as AustralianAI, AustralianCloud, AustralianTechnology, AustralianCompanies, or AustralianFinance.
- Comparison content has stronger commercial intent than general opinion posts.
- The required fact-verification step reduces inaccurate pricing and feature claims.
- The article format can support affiliate links, service enquiries, Shopify products, and sponsored placements once disclosure and editorial rules are established.

### Recommended publishing workflow

Research current pricing and features from official sources, prepare a verified fact table, draft the article with the skill template, complete an owner review, publish it to the website, then create a shortened Telegram post linking back to the article.

### Governance requirements

- Never invent pricing, features, user counts, rankings, or test results.
- Clearly disclose affiliate or sponsored relationships.
- Do not rank the owner's product first unless the evidence supports it.
- Keep factual claims and editorial opinion distinct.
- Record the source URL and verification date for every product compared.
- Recheck time-sensitive pricing before publication and scheduled refreshes.
